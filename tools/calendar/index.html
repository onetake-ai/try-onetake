Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

Opens your Calendar app, which asks you to confirm the subscription.

Subscribe in Apple Calendar
It didn't work? Add it by hand

On iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, Calendar (on older versions: Settings, Calendar).
  2. Tap Calendar Accounts (or Accounts), Add Account, Other, then Add Subscribed Calendar.
  3. Paste the calendar link below, tap Next, then Save.

On a Mac:

  1. Open the Calendar app, then the File menu, New Calendar Subscription.
  2. Paste the calendar link below and click Subscribe.
  3. Set Auto-refresh to Every hour, then click OK.

It didn't work? Add it by hand

In Outlook on the web or the new Outlook:

  1. Open the calendar, then click Add calendar.
  2. Choose Subscribe from web, paste the calendar link below and click Import.

In classic Outlook for Windows:

  1. Open the calendar. In the Home tab, click Add Calendar, then From Internet.
  2. Paste the calendar link below, click OK, then Yes.

  3. Paste the calendar link below and click Add calendar.
